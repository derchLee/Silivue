1. Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how Silivue (“Silivue,” “the app,” “we,” or “us”) handles information when you use the Silivue macOS application.

Short version: Silivue does not send your system-monitoring data to us. The app reads system information and stores settings and history locally on your Mac.

2. Information processed on your Mac

To provide its features, Silivue may read and process the following information locally:

  • CPU activity and total core count;
  • memory usage, pressure, compression, cache, and swap information;
  • network transfer rates, interface details, local network information, and the connected Wi-Fi network name when permission is granted;
  • disk capacity, usage, and available space;
  • battery charge, power source, and health information when available through public macOS interfaces; and
  • the macOS system thermal state.

If you open Performance Replay, Silivue may also use the public bundle identifiers of currently running applications to classify broad workload context, such as an IDE, terminal, local AI runtime, or container environment. This classification is performed only on your Mac, is shown as context rather than an attribution of resource use, and does not include process names, command contents, file paths, account information, or network destinations.

This information is used to display current metrics and historical charts, create on-device health summaries and anomaly explanations, provide local workload context, and trigger optional local notifications inside Silivue. It is not transmitted to the developer.

3. Local storage and retention

Silivue stores preferences in macOS user defaults and stores monitoring samples in a local database within your Mac’s application-support storage. The app automatically removes monitoring samples older than 30 days when it starts.

You can use the History settings in Silivue to prune old history or clear all stored history. Removing the app may not automatically remove all application-support files; macOS users can also remove Silivue’s local support data manually.

If you choose Export CSV, Silivue writes the selected history to a location you choose through the macOS save panel. Silivue does not upload or otherwise receive exported files.

4. Location permission and Wi-Fi names

macOS restricts access to the connected Wi-Fi network name behind Location Services. Silivue may therefore request location authorization so it can display the current Wi-Fi network name.

Silivue does not request, use, store, or transmit your geographic coordinates. If you deny location permission, other monitoring features continue to work, but the Wi-Fi network name may be unavailable. You can change permission at any time in macOS System Settings.

5. Data we collect

Silivue does not collect personal information or system-monitoring data from the app. The current version does not include:

  • developer-operated analytics or telemetry;
  • advertising SDKs or cross-app tracking;
  • accounts, cloud synchronization, or remote storage; or
  • in-app purchases or subscriptions.

Apple may independently process App Store downloads, purchases, diagnostics, or analytics according to Apple’s own policies and your device settings. That processing is controlled by Apple, not Silivue.

6. Sharing and sale

Because Silivue does not receive your personal information or monitoring data, we do not sell, rent, or share that data with advertisers, data brokers, or other third parties.

7. Security and your choices

Silivue uses the macOS App Sandbox and stores its data locally. No security method can eliminate every risk, so you should also protect your Mac with appropriate account, password, disk-encryption, and operating-system security settings.

You can control Silivue by disabling individual monitors, changing the refresh interval, revoking location permission, clearing history, disabling launch at login, or uninstalling the app.

8. Children’s privacy

Silivue is a general-purpose system utility and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. International privacy rights

Silivue does not maintain a developer-controlled database of user personal information. As a result, we generally have no remotely held personal data to access, correct, export, or delete. You remain in control of the information stored locally by the app on your Mac.

10. Changes to this policy

2. Mac에서 처리되는 정보

Silivue는 기능 제공을 위해 다음 정보를 기기에서 읽고 처리할 수 있습니다.

  • CPU 활동 및 전체 코어 수
  • 메모리 사용량, 압력, 압축, 캐시 및 스왑 정보

Performance Replay를 열면 Silivue는 실행 중인 앱의 공개 번들 식별자를 이용해 IDE, 터미널, 로컬 AI 런타임, 컨테이너 환경과 같은 넓은 범주의 작업 컨텍스트를 분류할 수 있습니다. 이 분류는 Mac에서만 수행되며 리소스 사용 원인으로 단정하지 않습니다. 프로세스 이름, 명령 내용, 파일 경로, 계정 정보 또는 네트워크 대상은 포함하지 않습니다.

이 정보는 현재 지표와 기록 차트 표시, 기기 내 상태 요약 및 이상 설명 생성, 로컬 작업 컨텍스트 제공, 사용자가 선택한 로컬 알림 전송에만 사용되며 개발자에게 전송되지 않습니다.

Silivue는 macOS 사용자 기본 설정에 환경설정을 저장하고 Mac의 응용 프로그램 지원 저장소 내 로컬 데이터베이스에 모니터링 샘플을 보관합니다. 앱은 시작할 때 30일이 지난 모니터링 샘플을 자동으로 삭제합니다.

Silivue의 기록 설정에서 오래된 기록을 정리하거나 저장된 모든 기록을 삭제할 수 있습니다. 앱을 제거해도 모든 응용 프로그램 지원 파일이 자동으로 삭제되지 않을 수 있으며, 사용자가 Silivue의 로컬 지원 데이터를 직접 삭제할 수도 있습니다.

CSV 내보내기를 선택하면 Silivue는 macOS 저장 패널에서 사용자가 지정한 위치에 선택된 기록을 기록합니다. Silivue는 내보낸 파일을 업로드하거나 수신하지 않습니다.
